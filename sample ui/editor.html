Review Summaries
Prof. Amara Osei Accept

Comprehensive and well-structured review of federated learning in medical imaging. The comparative analysis of privacy mechanisms is particularly valuable. Minor suggestions for strengthening the clinical validation discussion. Recommend acceptance with optional minor revisions.

Submitted: Aug 22, 2026 — Confidence: High
Dr. Kenji Tanaka Minor Revision

Strong contribution to the field. However, the section on communication efficiency lacks comparison with recent split learning approaches. Additionally, the dermatology case study needs more detail on dataset composition. These are addressable within a minor revision.

Submitted: Aug 24, 2026 — Confidence: High
Dr. Hans Müller Major Revision

The review covers important ground but has significant gaps. The taxonomy of federated learning approaches is outdated — missing 2025–2026 works on asynchronous FL. The privacy analysis does not address membership inference attacks. The clinical validation section needs substantial expansion with quantitative metrics.

Submitted: Aug 20, 2026 — Confidence: Medium
