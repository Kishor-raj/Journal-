Self-Supervised Representation Learning for Low-Resource Languages

Article TypeOriginal Research
SubmittedAug 5, 2026
Word Count8,960
Revision DeadlineAug 29, 2026
Abstract

This paper introduces a novel self-supervised pre-training approach for low-resource languages that leverages cross-lingual transfer from related high-resource languages. We propose a contrastive learning framework that learns shared representations across language families while preserving language-specific morphological features. Experiments on 12 low-resource languages demonstrate state-of-the-art performance on downstream NLP tasks including named entity recognition, sentiment analysis, and machine translation augmentation.

Editor's Revision Instructions: The reviewers identified significant issues with the evaluation methodology (Section 4.2) and the comparison with recent multilingual LLM approaches. Please address all reviewer comments point-by-point in your cover letter and submit a revised manuscript with tracked changes.
